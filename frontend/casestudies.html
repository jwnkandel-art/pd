Healthcare 01

Reducing Admin Burden at National Health Trust

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Clinical staff were spending over 3 hours daily on administrative queries, including appointment scheduling, policy lookups, and IT requests, which diverted time from patient care.

Deployed NexaAI Virtual Assistant reduced admin query volume by 67%, saving 2.1 hours per clinician per day and improving patient-facing care time by 40%.

Financial Services 02

Accelerating Compliance Onboarding at Global Bank

Banking & Financial Services

A Tier-1 bank's onboarding process for new hires took an average of 6 weeks due to fragmented systems, manual compliance training, and document-heavy workflows.

NexaAI's DEX platform reduced onboarding time from 6 weeks to 9 days, an 85% improvement, while achieving 100% compliance completion rates and a 4.7/5 new-hire satisfaction score.

Retail 03

Transforming Store Operations for International Retailer

Retail & Consumer Goods

With 12,000 frontline staff across 800 stores, the retailer struggled with inconsistent policy knowledge, slow escalation paths, and high training costs during peak seasons.

NexaAI's mobile-first AI assistant provided instant policy access to all frontline staff, cutting escalation rates by 54% and reducing seasonal training costs by £2.3M annually.

Technology 04

Rapid Prototype Delivery for SaaS Scale-up

Technology & SaaS

A fast-growing SaaS company needed to validate 12 product concepts in 3 months but lacked design resources. Traditional prototyping was costing 3 to 4 weeks per concept.

Using NexaAI's Prototyping Solution, all 12 concepts were built and user-tested in 6 weeks, 5 times faster than previous methods, enabling the team to ship the winning product within the quarter.

Public Sector 05

Digital Transformation for Government Agency

Government & Public Sector

A government department with 8,000 employees had a fragmented intranet with 2,300+ pages of unstructured content, resulting in low adoption (23%) and high IT support costs.

NexaAI's AI-powered DEX platform restructured content, added intelligent search, and deployed a virtual assistant, increasing intranet adoption to 87% and reducing IT support calls by 49%.

Manufacturing 06

Predictive Workforce Analytics for Global Manufacturer

Manufacturing & Engineering
